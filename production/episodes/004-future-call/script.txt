In this town, on your thirteenth birthday, your phone rings once. It's you, from the future. They tell you one thing. My sister's said, take the job in the city. She's rich now. My friend's said, say sorry to Dad. He did. I turned thirteen today. The phone rang. I picked it up. Silence. Then breathing. Then a voice that sounded exactly like me said: don't answer the phone. I looked at the screen. The call was coming from inside my house.
