## Narration

In this town, every kind thing you do drops a gold coin. Hold a door? One coin. Help someone up? Five. So everyone is kind… but only when they're watching for coins. Then there's old Mister Abe. He fixes bikes for free, and he never picks up his coins. People call him crazy. When he got sick, the whole town found out why. Behind his house was a mountain of gold, as tall as the clock tower. And a sign that said: for whoever needs it more. He never did it for the coins. That's why there were so many.
