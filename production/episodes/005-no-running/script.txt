In this town, running is illegal. Walking only. Even our races are speed-walking. Because if you run, the ground cracks under your feet. Grandma says the town is built on a sleeping giant, and fast footsteps wake him up. I never believed her. Today I was late for school. I looked left. I looked right. I ran. The ground shook. The street split open. A giant eye opened under the road. It looked right at me and said: bro. It's seven a.m. Then it went back to sleep. I still got detention.
