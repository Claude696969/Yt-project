In this town, you never fall asleep outside. Not on a bench. Not in the park. Not even for a minute. Because when the streetlights flicker, anyone sleeping outside is gone by morning. Nobody knows where they go. Last night, I saw my little brother asleep on the swings. The lights started flickering. I ran. I grabbed his arm. And then I heard the lights… whisper. Not him. We want the one who hasn't slept in three days. I haven't slept since Tuesday. I always wondered why the streetlights followed me home.
