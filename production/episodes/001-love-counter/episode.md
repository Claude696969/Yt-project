## Narration

In this town, everyone has a number above their head. It counts how many people love you. My mom has thirty. The mayor has nine hundred. Mine has always been zero. Kids laughed. Teachers looked away. I stopped going outside. Then one night the power went out, and a little girl was stuck in the dark, crying. I gave her my flashlight and walked her home. The next morning, my number said one. I smiled… until I saw hers. Her number had always been zero too. Now it said one. It was me.
